# PERDIMI — "They can smell it" (two models, hundreds of pigs)

**Length:** 19 s · 9:16 · 23 cuts · no product on screen
**Hook:** at 2.0 s every pig goes silent and turns its head towards the models. The models never look down.

## Look

- **Location:** US Midwest hog farm, stuck in 1974. Faded red barn with peeling paint, a galvanised grain silo,
  an Aermotor windmill, a rusted orange 1970s tractor, a sun-bleached 1972 pickup (pale green, rounded fenders), a hand-painted
  sign "HOGS – FEEDER PIGS – CASH", a rusted soda sign on the barn, galvanised troughs, barbed wire on wooden posts.
  These live in the **bokeh**: soft, recognisable, never sharp.
- **Light:** overcast late afternoon, low sun breaking through behind the barn, mist on the ground, a warm rim on the pigs' backs.
  Kodak 5219 feel: grain, lifted blacks, muted greens, rust oranges.
- **Ground:** deep wet mud, puddles reflecting the sky, straw trodden in.
- **Pigs:** hundreds, pink and spotted, packed tight, wet, steaming slightly.

## Wardrobe

- **Model A:** long black leather coat, sheer black lace bodysuit, pointed black stiletto boots, silver cuff, three silver rings,
  dark red lip, hair slicked back.
- **Model B:** ivory satin bias-cut gown (the mud has to show on it), black satin opera gloves, pearl drop earrings,
  pointed black slingbacks, loose dark hair.
- Both: eyes forward, no smile, a slow runway pace. They never acknowledge the pigs.

## Camera language

- **Two heights only:** **LOW** = pig eye level, about 40 cm off the ground, lens almost in the mud. **HUMAN** = 160 cm, shoulder-held.
- **Handheld throughout.** No gimbal smoothness, no tripod.
- **SETTLE** (the default move): push towards the target, overshoot it by about 10 %, over-correct back past it, then settle
  slightly off-centre and micro-reframe once. About 0.4 s from start to settled.
- **CATCH:** the operator is late. The subject is already moving, the frame chases it and finds it.
- **RACK:** focus pulls from foreground to subject mid-move.
- Lens: 35 mm for wides and two-shots, 85 mm for details, macro for eyes and texture. Shutter 1/50 for natural motion blur.

## Sound

- 0–2 s: loud, dense grunting and squealing, mud squelch, wind, a tractor idling far away.
- **2.0 s:** hard cut to near-silence. Only wind, and a low sub drone fading in.
- After 2 s: only the models' footsteps in mud (close-mic'd, wet, rhythmic), the creak of leather, satin rustle. The pigs stay silent.
- 17 s: one single grunt as the pig follows. Then silence.
- No music.

## Rules for the edit

- Cut on movement: every cut lands mid-step or mid-turn, never at rest.
- Alternate LOW and HUMAN. Never three of the same height in a row (shots 8–9 and 12–13 are the allowed LOW pairs).
- First frame has movement: the heel is already falling.
- Faces appear for 0.6 s at most until shot 22. The over-the-shoulder look is the only long face shot.
- Text: only at the end. Thin serif, white, lower third.
